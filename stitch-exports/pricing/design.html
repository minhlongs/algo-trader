What is the API rate limit? expand_more
Our standard rate limits are tiered by subscription level. Free users receive 10 requests per minute. Pro users benefit from 1,000 requests per minute with burst capability. Enterprise users have custom defined limits based on infrastructure load.
Is there a latency guarantee? expand_more
We provide a 99.99% uptime SLA for Pro and Enterprise tiers. For latency, Enterprise users can access direct fiber-optic cross-connects at our primary exchange hubs for sub-millisecond execution speeds.
Can I upgrade mid-cycle? expand_more
Yes. Upgrades are processed instantly. Your account will be charged a prorated amount for the remainder of the current billing cycle, and new tier benefits (like increased API limits) will be applied to your token immediately.
