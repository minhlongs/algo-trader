# DESIGN SYSTEM: Obsidian Quantum (Institutional Cyber-Quant)

## 1. Aesthetic Philosophy
Ultra-dense, low-latency institutional cyber-quant terminal engineered for quantitative researchers, algorithmic traders, and prop trading desks. Zero decorative fluff; pure mathematical rigor, high-contrast signal hierarchy, and micro-second visual clarity.

## 2. Color Palette Tokens
- **Background Canvas:** `#08090C` (Ultra-Deep Black)
- **Surface / Card Background:** `#0F1218`
- **Border / Outline:** `#1E2430` (1px sharp technical border)
- **Primary Accent (Signal Pulse):** `#00F2FE` (High-frequency Cyan)
- **Secondary Accent (Alpha Positive):** `#00E599` (Mint Green for PnL / Success)
- **Danger Accent (Kill-Switch / Drawdown):** `#FF3B30` (Neon Circuit-Breaker Red)
- **Muted Text / Secondary Labels:** `#8E9AA8`
- **Primary Text:** `#FFFFFF`

## 3. Typography
- **Headlines / Display:** `Space Grotesk`, `Plus Jakarta Sans`
- **Tabular Figures & Microstructure Numbers:** `JetBrains Mono`, `Roboto Mono`
- **Body & Explanatory Copy:** `Inter`, `Manrope`

## 4. UI Shape & Elevation
- **Corner Roundness:** `ROUND_FOUR` (4px radius across buttons, inputs, panels)
- **Elevation:** Flat with 1px `#1E2430` border; subtle outer cyan glow `#00F2FE` (0 0 12px rgba(0, 242, 254, 0.15)) on active cards.

## 5. Component Contracts
- **Kill-Switch:** High-priority saturated `#FF3B30` with high-contrast text and double-confirmation safety state.
- **Badges:** `PAPER` (Neutral slate), `SHADOW` (Amber glow), `LIVE` (Mint glow `#00E599`).
- **Telemetry Pills:** WebSocket latency indicator (< 12ms live green pulse).
