Is there a free trial for paid plans?
Yes. Every paid plan comes with a 7-day free trial. Cancel anytime before the trial ends and you won't be charged.
Can I cancel anytime?
Absolutely. Cancel anytime from your dashboard. You retain access until the end of your billing period.
Do you offer refunds?
We offer a 14-day refund policy on first-time Pro and Enterprise subscriptions. Master plan refunds are handled on a case-by-case basis.
