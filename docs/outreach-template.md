## 2. Features to Highlight (Demo Script)

### Opening (2 min)
"Let me show you CashClaw — we're building the Stripe of algo-trading. One platform, 52+ strategies, AI co-pilot, and enterprise-grade risk management."

### Demo Flow (10 min)

#### A. Dashboard Overview (2 min)
- Show real-time P&L tracking
- Highlight leaderboard (social proof)
- Point out WebSocket live data

**Talking point:** "Watch the price update in real-time — no refresh needed."

#### B. Strategy Marketplace (3 min)
- Browse 52+ strategies
- Show strategy performance metrics
- Demonstrate subscription flow

**Talking point:** "Each strategy has backtested performance data. You can subscribe with one click."

#### C. AI Co-pilot (3 min)
- Query: "What's my P&L on ETH this week?"
- Query: "Show me arbitrage opportunities"
- Query: "What's the market regime right now?"

**Talking point:** "Ask it anything in plain English. It pulls real data from your portfolio."

#### D. Risk Engine (2 min)
- Show drawdown protection
- Demonstrate Kelly sizing
- Highlight circuit breaker

**Talking point:** "Your portfolio is protected. If drawdown hits 5%, trading halts automatically."

## 3. Pricing Discussion Guide

### Objection Handling

**"It's too expensive"**
- "What are you paying for trading tools now?"
- "Our PRO tier pays for itself with one good trade"
- "Beta users get 50% off first month — $49 instead of $99"

**"I can build this myself"**
- "You could. But our 52+ strategies took 18 months to build and test."
- "Plus risk management, marketplace, and AI co-pilot."
- "What's your time worth at $X/hour?"

**"I don't trade enough to justify it"**
- "Paper trading is free — no commitment needed."
- "Start with 3 strategies on the FREE tier."
- "Upgrade when you see results."

**"I need to think about it"**
- "Totally understand. Can I send you a 2-min demo video?"
- "Beta spots are limited — want me to hold one for you?"

### Tier Recommendations

| User Profile | Recommended Tier | Why |
|--------------|-----------------|-----|
| Casual trader, paper only | FREE | No risk, try before you buy |
| Serious trader, 1-2 exchanges | PRO ($99) | Full strategy access, risk engine |
| Power user, multiple strategies | ENTERPRISE ($299) | API access, priority support |
| Institution, custom needs | MASTER ($999) | Unlimited, white-glove support |

## 4. Onboarding Checklist (New Customer)

### Pre-Onboarding (Before Call)
- [ ] Verify account created
- [ ] Check API keys configured (if applicable)
- [ ] Review trading goals from signup form
- [ ] Prepare custom demo based on their use case

### Onboarding Call (15-30 min)

**0-5 min: Welcome**
- [ ] Confirm name, trading experience, goals
- [ ] Show dashboard, verify login works
- [ ] Confirm email notifications working

**5-15 min: Setup**
- [ ] Walk through API key setup (if needed)
- [ ] Enable paper trading mode
- [ ] Pick 1-2 strategies from marketplace
- [ ] Run first paper trade together

**15-25 min: Features**
- [ ] Demo AI co-pilot queries
- [ ] Show risk engine settings
- [ ] Explain leaderboard + P&L tracking
- [ ] Discuss go-live timeline

**25-30 min: Next Steps**
- [ ] Schedule 1-week check-in
- [ ] Share Discord/support channel
- [ ] Confirm billing working
- [ ] Send recap email with links

### Post-Onboarding (Day 1)
- [ ] Send welcome email with quick-start guide
- [ ] Add to beta-user Discord channel
- [ ] Schedule day-7 check-in reminder
- [ ] Log any issues in support ticket system

### Week 1 Check-in
- [ ] Review paper trading results
- [ ] Discuss strategy performance
- [ ] Address any issues/questions
- [ ] Gather initial feedback (1-3 questions)

### Week 4 Review
- [ ] Monthly feedback survey
- [ ] Discuss go-live readiness
- [ ] Review pricing tier fit
- [ ] Collect testimonial/case study (if positive)
